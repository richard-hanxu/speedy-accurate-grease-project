## Per-event notes

1. **Trend / Panamanian tanker (16 Sep).** Nobody gives a UKMTO clock time. UKMTO's unattributed notices (an outbound tanker hit on 16 Sep and a 17 Sep "security incident" 16 nm NE of Khasab) came before the IRGC Navy statement, but the IRGC (Rear Adm. Azmaei, via IRNA/state TV) was the first to name *Trend*. That claim came out on Thursday night 17 Sep, Tehran time. AFP says it is unclear whether UKMTO's Khasab incident is the same ship. The "unidentified Panamanian tanker" is most likely UKMTO's 16 Sep outbound case, but I could not confirm this. Pattern: neutral but anonymous first, then a named claim from Iran about 1 day later.
2. **STI Steadfast (18 Sep).** The only hard timestamp is Reuters at 07:28Z on 18 Sep, quoting UKMTO (fire extinguished, crew safe). No public source I found names STI Steadfast. The name is most likely from a later Windward or Lloyd's List identification.
3. **Al Maryah (20 Sep).** This is the clearest case of a government channel beating UKMTO. India's Directorate of Maritime Administration (9 Indian crew) put out details that Indian outlets carried by 07:13Z on 21 Sep. UKMTO's "delayed report" of an outbound LPG tanker hit by debris came on the afternoon of 21 Sep. ADNOC put out no statement that I could find.
4. **LR Stephanie (21–22 Sep).** UKMTO first (cited military authorities) on the morning of Mon 21 Sep, with wires within about 1–4 h. The ship was named by Marisks via Reuters about 24 h later. The Wikipedia date (22 Sep) is probably the identification date, not the incident date.
5. **Cape Dao (23 Sep).** UKMTO first (incident about 06:30Z). Oman MSC and India (embassy, MEA) added the death toll within hours, and the union named the victim later. An IRGC link appeared only through unverified social-media footage. The sinking (14 Oct) was reported by Reuters and gCaptain.
6. **REMUS 600 (27 Sep).** This was a claim-only event, and the origin is Iranian (an IRGC Navy statement on state TV). It was on the wires by about 13:16Z at the latest. CENTCOM denied it shortly afterwards via Al Jazeera. No neutral source came before the claim.
7. **Al Funtas (28 Sep).** The ship transited with AIS off and did not report itself, so UKMTO learned of the strike late from a third party. Lloyd's List named it on 29 Sep. Lag from strike to named report: roughly 12–18 h.
8. **Mersin Prosperity / Sinbad / Al Ruwais (29 Sep).** UKMTO published delayed reports, Vanguard Tech named the ships, and Marisks/Reuters added the AIS-off detail. Iran made no direct claim; Fars and an IRGC spokesman gave a threatening framing instead. Strike times are unknown.
9. **Kazimah III (1 Oct).** UKMTO got a third-party report at 17:50Z. Anadolu says Fars had reported "earlier" that a 2.5m-bbl supertanker was on fire on an "unauthorised route", so Iranian media may have been first, but I could not get Fars's timestamp. Vanguard named the ship the next day.
10. **Uhud (2 Oct).** The strike was at 11:22Z (master to UKMTO). Iran International relayed it by 16:11Z (about 5 h later). The name comes from Vanguard via Seatrade only. JMIC dates it to 3 Oct.
11. **Ghana Prosperity (3 Oct).** A delayed UKMTO report from a verified source. The name came from industry sources (Seatrade/Pole Star) 2–3 days later. No time found.
12. **Lipsi / Gas Vela / Cameroon Prosperity (4 Oct).** UKMTO first for all three (Gas Vela 17:16Z, Cameroon Prosperity 19:07Z). Lipsi stands out: Pakistan's NAVAREA IX navigational warning named it the same day, before most media. Vanguard also named it. Cyprus Mail carried it at 09:05Z on 5 Oct.
13. **Maran Gas Mystras (5 Oct).** UKMTO 156-26 timed it at 16:37Z on 5 Oct. The Greek Ministry of Shipping and the owner confirmed the crew was safe. Some Greek outlets say "Sunday" (4 Oct), which conflicts with UKMTO's date.

## Gaps and cautions
- I could not open UKMTO's own warning PDFs (403), so every UKMTO time is second-hand.
- I found no X/Telegram OSINT posts with timestamps. Those channels (and Windward's daily tracker) may have been first for several events, but I could not verify this.
- I did not find Tasnim or Sepah News originals. Iranian claims are traced through IRNA, Press TV, Fars and state TV as quoted by wires.
