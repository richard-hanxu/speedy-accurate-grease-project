# News provenance – Batch 5 (13–20 Oct 2026)

## How timestamps were established

- **UKMTO PDF creation time.** I downloaded the primary UKMTO warning PDFs from `ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/` into the scratchpad (`ukmto/` subfolder). Each one carries a `/CreationDate` in its metadata. This is a close proxy for when the warning was issued.
- **UKMTO X post time.** I decoded the post times from the X status IDs (Twitter snowflake: `(id>>22)+1288834974657` ms). Across 7 warnings, the X post lagged the PDF by **4–28 min**, with a typical lag of about 5–13 min.
- **Other outlets.** Times come from page bylines and metadata. Where an outlet does not state its timezone, I say so.
- **Tasnim times.** Tasnim times are assumed to be Tehran time (UTC+3:30). This is not confirmed on the page. The assumption is consistent with Press TV's UTC-stamped copy of the same statement appearing about 50 min later.

- **Ship identities.** The UKMTO weekly overview of 16 Oct names GFS GISELLE (172) and AL SAADIYAT (173). It withholds the names for 170 and 171. Vanguard Tech's identification of those two as GFS Pride and Alfonso is the only one available and is not official.
- **Dates.** The MPA dates the Giselle strike 14 Oct Singapore time, which matches 1640Z on 13 Oct.
- **IRGC claim timing.** The IRGC's mine claim (images credited to Mehr, plus a Fars post) came on the "morning" of 14 Oct, local time. That is after UKMTO and Reuters on the evening of 13 Oct. I could not pin a UTC time on it.
- **CENTCOM rebuttal.** CENTCOM's rebuttal (IRGC projectile; the ship sank under tow) came about 46 h after the UKMTO alert.
- **Lag for named-ship news.** For trading purposes, the actionable "ship hit" headline was Reuters at 19:24Z, citing UKMTO, about 28 min after UKMTO's PDF. Named-vessel news (MPA, Vanguard) came 12–20 h later.

**Rows 5a/5b (Jubail).**
- **Two warnings.** These are two separate UKMTO warnings with different times (0900Z and 1922Z on 16 Oct), different sources (Company Security Officer vs. Master) and different distances (68 vs. 78 nm). Reuters could not tell whether they describe the same incident.
- **Where the event date comes from.** The "17 Oct" date in the brief reflects when the warnings were published, not when the incidents happened.
- **"New attack type" framing.** An RPG fired from a small boat that far up the Gulf is new for this conflict, and the trade press framed it that way (Maritime Executive). I did not find UKMTO's own wording calling it a "new attack type".

**Row 6 (20 Oct mine claims).**
- **Iranian media came first.** Iranian state media is the only original source. The two-tanker statement came in the morning; the supertanker statement came in the evening via Fars.
- **CENTCOM's denial channel.** The CENTCOM spokesman's denial reached markets first through Al Jazeera Arabic breaking, then Newsquawk at 14:40Z. That is about 4.5 h after the Tasnim claim.
- **No neutral corroboration.** No UKMTO or JMIC warning corroborates either claim. No UKMTO product dated 19–20 Oct was found in my probe of 178-26 / 179-26.

**Row 7 (tug).**
- **Date correction.** UKMTO dates the tug incident **18 Oct**, not 20 Oct. The event list should be corrected.
- **Lead time over the wires.** UKMTO's X post was about 2 h ahead of the first wire copies.

**Row 8 (MQ-9).**
- **First outlet unknown.** I could not identify which Iranian outlet published first. Candidates are Sepah News, IRIB and Mehr.
- **Time bound.** The NewsCord aggregation timestamp of 06:02Z is the hard upper bound.
- **No US response found.** I found no CENTCOM response.

**Other 14–20 Oct items.**
- **No UKMTO attacks on 14–15 Oct.** UKMTO numbering runs 170→177 across 13–18 Oct. That means no other UKMTO-reported attacks occurred 14–15 Oct beyond the late-issued 172/173.
- **Unverified IRGC claims.** IRGC made further unverified mine claims on 16 Oct and 17 Oct (row 10).
- **UKMTO weekly summary.** The UKMTO weekly of 16 Oct says 12 attacks occurred in the week to 14 Oct. It adds that most attacked vessels now refuse to report, so many attacks go unseen by UKMTO.
