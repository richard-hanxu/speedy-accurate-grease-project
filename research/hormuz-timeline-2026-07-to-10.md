# Strait of Hormuz: Major Events, 20 Jul – 20 Oct 2026

Compiled 2026-10-20 from web reporting (Wikipedia crisis/campaign pages, Al Jazeera, CNN, CNBC, Lloyd's List Intelligence, CBS, France 24, straits.live). Dates are event dates (UTC-ish, as reported). **Iranian and US claims frequently conflict. Treat claims marked (claim) as unverified.** Prices are as reported in the news, not checked against tick data. Verify them in Databento before using any of these as trigger timestamps.

★ = probable market-moving / "trigger" candidate for the markout study.

## Background (pre-window)
- **28 Feb**: US–Israel war on Iran begins. Iran closes the strait to "unfriendly" shipping.
- **8 Apr**: Ceasefire. **13 Apr**: Islamabad talks fail, US imposes naval blockade of Iranian ports.
- **27 May**: OFAC sanctions Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority (PGSA).
- **16–17 Jun**: Islamabad MoU signed (interim peace deal, Hormuz reopening, PGSA passage-request regime).
- **8 Jul** ★: Truce collapses. Iran hits commercial ships, the US strikes Iran, and Iran strikes US Gulf bases. Trump says the deal "is over".
- **14 Jul** ★: US reinstates the blockade of Iranian ports (CENTCOM announces 15 Jul). Three Liberian-flagged ships hit (*Stolt Magnesium*, *Mombasa B*, *Al Bahyah*).
- **15 Jul**: CENTCOM strikes dozens of Iranian military sites near Hormuz.
- **18 Jul** ★: Iran formally suspends its Islamabad MoU commitments. Brent above $86 as traffic hits a 3-week low.

## July (20–31)
- **20 Jul** ★: Greek-owned tankers *Kavomaleas* (seized and towed into Iranian waters) and *Acheloos* struck off Oman. Kuwaiti tanker *Kaifan* also hit.
- **23 Jul** ★: Brent tops **$100.69**, then closes at $96.78 on 24 Jul.
- **24 Jul**: Tanker *Lavine* disabled by US forces (blockade enforcement).
- **26 Jul**: Iran's foreign ministry accuses the US of violating the MoU and says it is negotiating a safe-passage route with Oman, not the US.
- **31 Jul** ★: LNG carrier *GasLog Shanghai* (Bermuda) disabled while leaving with Qatari LNG. This was the first LNG hit in the window and matters for TTF/JKM.
- **July totals (Lloyd's List Intelligence)**: 160 westbound non-Iranian transits, including 72 crude tankers.

## August
- **3 Aug**: Bulk carrier *Minoan Pioneer* hit, with one engineer missing and a 10 km+ oil slick.
- **5–7 Aug** ★: Iran says it has reached a Hormuz shipping-route agreement with Oman. The joint statement is "in final drafting".
- **8 Aug**: UAE says an Iranian missile hit an ADNOC-owned tanker.
- **10 Aug**: Brent ~$84 (Oct contract) as Iran's demands cloud reopening.
- **11 Aug**: A US MH-60 fires Hellfires at the Panama-flagged *Vela Nova* (blockade runner) in the Gulf of Oman.
- **12 Aug** ★: Brent +2% to ~$90 after attacks. Only 10 transits on 11 Aug vs. ~130 pre-war.
- **13–14 Aug**: ADNOC tankers *Navig8 Messi* and *Tarif* hit by drones. Bulk carrier *Al Watan* hit.
- **15–16 Aug** ★: Traffic collapses. Kpler counts 5 transits on 15 Aug and **0 on 16 Aug** (vs. 31 the prior weekend).
- **16–17 Aug** ★: US–Iran MoU formally expires.
- **18 Aug**: *Minoan Dignity* attacked, chief engineer killed.
- **Aug (date unclear)**: PGSA begins publishing a "banned vessel" list (~56 ships by early Sept).
- **21 Aug**: CENTCOM says it has helped ~1,300 vessels and 660M bbl transit since early May, mostly via the southern/Omani corridor.
- **24 Aug**: Tanker *Metro Venetian* disabled.
- **25 Aug** ★: US confirms the Navy has cleared mines from the Traffic Separation Scheme (100+ suspected mines found). Trump threatens to destroy any Iranian mine-laying vessels. Kuwaiti tanker *Al Salam II* hit.
- **25–26 Aug** ★: Oman and Iran announce a "framework" for a temporary joint navigational corridor. Iran says full reopening still depends on the US honoring the MoU.
- **29 Aug**: Kuwaiti tanker *Burgan* hit and abandoned.
- **30 Aug** ★: US strikes Larak Island military sites over mine-laying. IRGC retaliates against US bases in Jordan.
- **31 Aug** ★: Saudi tanker *Sidr* hit (2 killed). *Senegal Prosperity* abandoned. IRGC says a VLCC hit two mines on an "unauthorized route".
- **August totals (Lloyd's List Intelligence)**: 346 non-Iranian transits (191 westbound), a post-conflict high. Crude tankers rose to 112 from 72. War-risk premiums reached ~2.25% per 7-day window (vs. ~0.125% pre-crisis). VLCC MEG–China earnings hit a record ~$760k/day.

## September
- **2 Sep**: IRGC says it struck two tankers on an "unauthorized route".
- **5 Sep** ★: Major escalation. The US attacks three Iranian ships, including a tanker near Kharg Island. Iran fires ballistic missiles at a US carrier and destroyer and claims hits on three tankers. Mohsen Rezaei declares a "prohibited zone" from the US blockade line to the loading ports.
- **6 Sep**: Energy Sec. Chris Wright says Navy escorts will continue until Iran backs down.
- **7 Sep** ★: Kpler reports no large ships transited since early September. A Saudi-loaded tanker is blocked from leaving.
- **8 Sep** ★: Brent rises to ~$99 on a report of a second undisclosed Iranian attack on US Navy ships.
- **9 Sep**: *Hercules Star* hit off Dubai (1 killed). *New Andros* hit by a drone in Iraqi waters.
- **13 Sep**: Tanker *El Gaia* hit north of Musandam (2 killed). The IRGC says it was a mine, the US says a drone.
- **16–23 Sep**: Steady attacks: *Trend*, *STI Steadfast*, LPG carrier *Al Maryah*, *LR Stephanie*. Bulk carrier *Cape Dao* hit on 23 Sep (it later sank).
- **25 Sep** ★: Araghchi floats a **7-day plan** via Qatar. The US would lift the blockade, waive oil sanctions, release frozen assets and agree a ceasefire on all fronts including Lebanon. Iran would reopen the strait on day 6 and start talks on day 7.
- **26 Sep** ★: **Trump rejects the 7-day plan**. WSJ reports he expects to resume strikes after the November midterms.
- **27 Sep**: IRGC says it captured a US REMUS 600 underwater drone and declares the strait closed to unauthorized access.
- **28 Sep** ★: Brent jumps above **$107** on the rejection.
- **27–30 Sep** ★: Flows spike. Kpler shows 19.5–22.5 mb/d on 27–29 Sep and a 7-day average of 18.3 mb/d on 30 Sep, above the pre-war 17.1 mb/d, driven by Saudi loadings and the US-escorted ADNOC ship-to-ship "shuttle". This prompts "flows top pre-war levels" headlines (CNBC, 30 Sep).
- **28 Sep–4 Oct**: Kpler counts 10 tankers struck (*Al Funtas*, *Mersin Prosperity*, *Sinbad*, *Al Ruwais*, ...).
- **September summary**: Non-Iranian Gulf oil flows reached >81% of pre-war. It was the first month since 2013 with **zero Iranian crude loadings** (Kpler), showing the US blockade is effective.

## October (1–20)
- **1–5 Oct**: Daily strikes: *Kazimah III* (abandoned), *Uhud*, *Ghana Prosperity*, *Lipsi* (disabled), LPG carrier *Gas Vela*, LNG carrier *Maran Gas Mystras* (5 Oct).
- **4 Oct** ★: Speaker Ghalibaf says the strait stays closed until the US meets seven conditions from the June agreement.
- **5 Oct**: Aramco's Nasser estimates ~3 billion bbl lost globally. ICC warns grain prices could rise up to 80%.
- **6–7 Oct**: *On Peace* attacked (12 injured). *Acers* hit off Qatar. IRGC adviser Naqdi says Iran will close "illegal" routes.
- **7–8 Oct** ★: Iran says it and Oman have agreed coordinates for designated "safe" transit routes. Brent ~$105 on record tanker attacks, with only 7 tankers transiting on 6 Oct.
- **8 Oct**: ~20.8M bbl transit in one day.
- **9–10 Oct**: *Gem No. 2* (fire, off UAE) and *Valthera* hit.
- **11 Oct** ★: **Heaviest day of attacks**. Six-plus ships hit: *Petro Dolphin* abandoned with 2 missing, *Angola Prosperity*, *Ever Vict*, *Kition M*, *DP World Kaveri*, VLCC *Al Maqam*. On 10–11 Oct, US forces also disabled the bulk carrier *Ocean Molica*. JMIC keeps the threat at "severe" (in place since 8 Oct). Flows fall to 2.9M bbl, the low of the month. Rezaei names the US–UAE oil shuttle as a target and claims 5–6 ships hit nightly (claim).
- **12 Oct**: CENTCOM says it helped move >19M bbl of Gulf crude.
- **13 Oct** ★: Four ships hit, including the container ship *GFS Pride* and *Al Saadiyat*. Iran's Navy announces new operational units east of the strait. Brent closes at $105.69 and WTI at $94.14.
- **14 Oct** ★: *GFS Pride* **sinks**. Iran says it was a mine, CENTCOM says an Iranian projectile. US official says Iran has shifted tactics to unarmed spotters in dhows. Brent ~$106.58.
- **16 Oct**: UKMTO says traffic is ~70% below pre-war, with 12 attacks in 7 days. Brent ~$105.80. CENTCOM's Cooper claims 400 ships and 100M bbl moved in the past week (claim).
- **16 Oct**: First RPG-from-skiff attack on a tanker, 78 nm NE of Jubail (UKMTO warning issued 17 Oct; the trade press calls it a new tactic).
- **17 Oct**: Both the US and Iran claim control of the strait.
- **18 Oct**: Tug *JIF Barracuda* hit by two projectiles off Khor Fakkan (UKMTO 177-26).
- **18 Oct** ★: Araghchi says the 7-day plan is "still on the table" and Iran is reviewing US comments. IMF PortWatch records **4 transits** (vs. 85/day baseline). Rubio claims shipping is at 80–100% of pre-war, which the data does not support.
- **20 Oct** ★: IRGC claims 2–3 tankers were set ablaze by mines on an unauthorized route, including a supertanker with >2M bbl (claim). CENTCOM calls this false. IRGC also claims an MQ-9 shootdown (its "54th", claim). Brent ~$109.

## Observations for the project
- **Recurring trigger types**: (1) attacks/sinkings, especially multi-ship days and LNG hits; (2) diplomatic headlines (MoU suspension/expiry, Iran–Oman corridor, the 7-day plan and its rejection); (3) US kinetic action (blockade reinstatement, Larak and Kharg strikes); (4) flow-data releases (Kpler, Windward, Lloyd's, PortWatch); (5) official claims that contradict the data (Trump, Rubio, CENTCOM vs. IRGC).
- **Claim vs. confirmation lag**: IRGC claims often come first (Tasnim, IRNA, Telegram), followed by UKMTO/CENTCOM confirmation or denial. This is a natural test case for the "which source is first" question in the 03 Oct meeting notes.
- **Data disagreement**: Transit counts differ by tracker (Kpler, Windward, PortWatch, Lloyd's) partly because escorted ships run with AIS off, so pick one source for consistency.
- **Price oddity to check**: Reports put Brent at ~$100 on 23 Jul but ~$84–90 in mid-August. Confirm against futures data, which may reflect a contract roll or a genuine sell-off.
