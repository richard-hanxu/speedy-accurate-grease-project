# Oil Markets Research Project

## Main Objectives
1. Identify “trigger” events which significantly move the oil markets
- Top-down approach looks at major new events first and checks the market afterwards, bottom-up looks at large price movements and tries to connect them with external events
2. Calculate the markout/market potential across futures/equities/options contracts after these events
- How fast is the new fair value realized after the event
3. Measure which markets move fastest/slowest
On our side we should also measure how fast it takes for our orders to reach the corresponding exchanges
